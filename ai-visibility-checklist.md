# Payflow SA AI Visibility Checklist

## Entity identity

- Brand: Payflow SA
- Canonical domain: https://mypayflowsa.co.za/
- Market: South Africa
- Category: payroll and workforce management software

## Crawlability

- Public pages should remain accessible without login.
- `robots.txt` should allow OAI-SearchBot and normal search crawlers.
- `sitemap.xml` should list canonical public pages.
- Avoid accidental `noindex` on commercial and knowledge pages.

## Evidence standards

Only publish claims that can be supported by the product, documentation, customer evidence, or authoritative third-party sources. Do not invent certifications, customer counts, security guarantees, compliance claims, integrations, or statutory capabilities.

## Content standards

Each knowledge page should answer a specific user problem directly, define relevant South African payroll terminology, explain what Payflow SA actually supports, and link to related product or FAQ pages.

## Authority signals to build

- Consistent company and product naming
- Clear About and Contact information
- Founder/company identity where appropriate
- Product documentation
- Genuine customer stories and testimonials
- Relevant South African business and software listings
- Legitimate partnerships and references
- Original educational material

## Measurement

Track branded searches, indexed pages, Search Console queries, referring domains, ChatGPT referral traffic, and whether Payflow SA is cited or mentioned for target questions. AI search placement is not guaranteed by any single technical change.
