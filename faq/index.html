Payflow SA Frequently Asked Questions

What is Payflow SA?

Payflow SA is payroll software designed for South African SMEs. It helps employers manage employee information, capture hours, run payroll, review totals and generate payslips.

Is Payflow SA built for South Africa?

Yes. The workflow uses South African payroll terminology, Rand values, employee records, payslips and payroll reporting.

Can I manage payroll without spreadsheets?

Payflow SA is designed to provide a structured payroll workflow so businesses do not have to rely entirely on disconnected spreadsheets.

Can I set it up myself?

Yes. Businesses can add their company, departments, employees, pay types and payroll details. Optional setup assistance may also be available.

Does Payflow SA replace a tax practitioner?

No. It is a payroll calculation and workflow aid. Employers remain responsible for accurate information and applicable tax, labour and submission obligations.

Can Payflow SA generate payslips?

Yes. The website describes payslip generation and export as part of the payroll workflow.

How do I start?

Use the website's Start My Free Month or WhatsApp call-to-action to contact Payflow SA and begin the onboarding process.
