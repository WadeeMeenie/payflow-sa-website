Payflow SA Pricing

Payflow SA currently offers two monthly payroll plans for South African businesses. The first month is free for new customers according to the current website offer.

PlanPriceIncludes
Timesheets to PayslipsR599/monthEmployee setup, hours, payroll run, payroll review, payslip output and payroll history.
Full Payroll AccessR899/monthEverything in R599 plus EMP201 summary, IRP5 certificates, EMP501 reconciliation, UI19 declaration, ETI breakdown and full report access.

Optional setup help

The current website also offers optional once-off setup assistance at R299, where Payflow SA helps set up the company and employees using information supplied by the customer.

Contract

The website describes the service as monthly with no contract and cancellation at any time.
