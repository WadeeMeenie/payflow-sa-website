Payflow SA Payroll Features

Payflow SA is built to give South African SMEs a simpler payroll workflow than relying on disconnected spreadsheets and manual documents.

Core workflow

  • Employee setup: organise company, department and employee information.
  • Hours and pay inputs: capture the information needed for a payroll run.
  • Payroll processing: run payroll and review totals before finalising.
  • Payslips: produce payslip output for employees.
  • Payroll history: keep payroll runs organised for later review.
  • Reports: the full plan provides access to available reports including EMP201 summary, IRP5 certificates, EMP501 reconciliation, UI19 declaration and ETI breakdown as represented by the current product offering.

Designed for mobile use

Payflow SA is delivered as an Android application and is intended to keep common payroll tasks accessible from a phone.
