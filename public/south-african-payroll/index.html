South African Payroll Software for SMEs

Payflow SA is designed around the payroll workflow of South African employers. The platform helps businesses manage employee records, capture hours, calculate payroll inputs, review totals and generate payslips.

Why South African payroll needs local context

South African employers may need to deal with PAYE, UIF, SDL, ETI, leave, overtime, payslips and other employment and tax requirements depending on their circumstances. Payroll software can reduce repetitive administration, but employers must still verify the outputs that apply to their workforce.

What Payflow SA helps with

  • Employee and company payroll information
  • Hours and timesheet capture
  • Payroll processing and review
  • Payslip generation and export
  • Payroll reports on supported plans

Is Payflow SA a replacement for a tax practitioner?

No. Payflow SA is a calculation and workflow aid. Businesses remain responsible for their payroll, tax and labour obligations and should obtain professional advice where required.

Who can use it?

Payflow SA is aimed at South African SMEs, business owners, payroll administrators and bookkeepers who want an easier alternative to managing payroll entirely through spreadsheets.
