About Payflow SA

Payroll software built for South African businesses.

Payflow SA helps small and medium-sized businesses organise employee information, capture hours, run payroll, review totals and generate payslips through a practical payroll workflow.

Built for South Africa

Payflow SA focuses on practical payroll workflows used by South African SMEs, including Rand values, employee records, timesheets and payroll reporting.

Who it's for

  • Small and medium-sized business owners
  • Employers managing their own payroll
  • Payroll administrators
  • Bookkeepers and teams needing a simpler workflow

A practical tool

The product is designed to make routine payroll work easier to organise and review, while keeping employers responsible for the accuracy and compliance of their payroll.

Responsible use

Software helps. Employers remain responsible.

Payflow SA is a payroll calculation and workflow aid. Employers remain responsible for accurate information, reviewing payroll results and meeting applicable tax, labour and submission obligations.
