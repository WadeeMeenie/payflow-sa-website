About Payflow SA

Payflow SA is payroll software designed for South African small and medium-sized businesses. It helps employers organise employee information, capture hours, run payroll, review totals and generate payslips.

Built for South African businesses

Payflow SA focuses on practical payroll workflows used by South African SMEs, including Rand values, employee records, timesheets and payroll reporting.

Who Payflow SA is for

  • Small and medium-sized business owners
  • Employers managing their own payroll
  • Payroll administrators
  • Bookkeepers and businesses that need a simpler payroll workflow

Important responsibility

Payflow SA is a payroll calculation and workflow aid. Employers remain responsible for accurate information, reviewing payroll results and meeting applicable tax, labour and submission obligations.
