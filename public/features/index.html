Payflow SA Payroll Features

Payflow SA brings common SME payroll tasks into one Android application, helping employers move from hours and employee information to reviewed payroll totals and payslips.

Employee management

Add and manage company employees, departments, pay types and payroll details from one place.

Timesheets and hours

Capture employee hours so payroll inputs are organised before payroll is processed.

Payroll review

Run payroll calculations and review totals before finalising the workflow. Accurate source information and human review remain important.

Payslips

Generate and export employee payslips as part of the payroll workflow.

Reports

Supported plans provide access to additional payroll reports. Check the current pricing section for plan-specific availability.

South African SME focus

Payflow SA is designed for South African employers and uses Rand values and local payroll terminology.
