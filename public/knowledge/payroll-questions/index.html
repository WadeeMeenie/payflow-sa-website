Common South African Payroll Questions

This guide answers practical questions South African small and medium-sized businesses commonly face when organising employee information, hours, payroll runs, payslips and payroll records.

What information is normally needed before running payroll?
Businesses generally need accurate employee information and the pay inputs relevant to the payroll period, such as hours, rates and other applicable earnings or deductions. The exact information depends on the employer's payroll process.
Why should payroll be reviewed before it is finalised?
Payroll depends on the accuracy of employee records and pay inputs. A review gives the employer an opportunity to identify incorrect hours, rates, employee details or other inputs before relying on the result.
What is a payslip?
A payslip is a payroll document provided to an employee showing information about their pay for a period. Employers should ensure the information presented is accurate and appropriate for their circumstances.
Can payroll software replace an employer's payroll responsibilities?
No. Software can organise calculations and workflow, but the employer remains responsible for accurate records, reviewing results and meeting applicable employment, tax, reporting and submission obligations.
Why are employee hours important in payroll?
For businesses where employees are paid according to hours or shifts, accurate time information can be an important input into payroll. A consistent timesheet process helps reduce avoidable manual errors.
What should a small business look for in payroll software?
Look for a workflow that matches the business's needs, supports the required employee and payroll records, makes results easy to review, produces the necessary documents and fits the people responsible for payroll.
Is mobile payroll useful for small businesses?
It can be useful when owners or payroll administrators need access to routine payroll tasks away from a desktop. Payflow SA is delivered as an Android application for this purpose.
Does Payflow SA provide payroll reports?
The current Payflow SA offering lists reports including EMP201 summary, IRP5 certificates, EMP501 reconciliation, UI19 declaration and ETI breakdown on the full plan. Customers should verify the current product offering before relying on a specific report or statutory workflow.

Use this guide with official requirements

Payroll rules and employer obligations can change. Payflow SA provides software and workflow assistance; it is not a substitute for current guidance from the relevant South African authorities or professional advice where required.
