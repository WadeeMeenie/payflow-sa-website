South African Payroll: A Practical Overview

Understand the basic workflow from employee information and pay inputs to reviewed payroll records and payslips.

Payroll is the process of turning employee information and pay inputs into calculated pay, payroll records and payslips. For a small business, the workflow usually includes maintaining employee records, capturing hours or other pay inputs, reviewing calculations and producing payroll documentation.

Why payroll software helps SMEs

Payroll software can reduce repetitive spreadsheet work by keeping employee information, hours, payroll runs and payslip output in one workflow. Payflow SA is designed around this workflow for South African SMEs.

A typical Payflow SA workflow

  1. Set up the company and employee information.
  2. Capture hours, rates and applicable pay inputs.
  3. Run the payroll calculation.
  4. Review totals before finalising.
  5. Generate or export payslips and available payroll reports.

South African context

South African employers may have tax, employment and reporting obligations that depend on their circumstances. Payflow SA is a payroll workflow and calculation aid, not a substitute for professional advice or the employer's responsibility to verify information and submissions.
