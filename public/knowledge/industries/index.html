Who Payflow SA Is Designed For

Payroll workflows for South African SMEs where employee hours, rates, records and payslips are recurring administrative tasks.

Examples

  • Contractors and subcontractors
  • Construction teams
  • Tender-based companies
  • Workshops
  • Cleaning companies
  • Security companies
  • Small retail teams
  • Service businesses
  • Other admin-heavy SMEs

Why these businesses can benefit

These businesses often need to turn hours and employee information into repeatable payroll records and payslips. Payflow SA puts those tasks into one Android workflow so owners and payroll administrators can review the result before finalising.
