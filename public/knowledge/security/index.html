Payflow SA Security and Responsible Payroll Use

Practical guidance for handling payroll information responsibly while keeping employer responsibilities clear.

Payroll information can be sensitive. Payflow SA is designed to organise payroll information and processing in an Android application, but the public website does not claim that the product eliminates every security or compliance risk.

What customers should do

  • Use strong account credentials and keep access limited to people who need it.
  • Enter accurate employee and payroll information.
  • Review payroll totals before finalising a run.
  • Keep exported payroll documents in appropriately controlled locations.
  • Keep the application and Android device software up to date.
  • Use professional or legal advice where a payroll or employment question requires it.

Employer responsibility

Payroll software is a tool. Employers remain responsible for the accuracy of their records, the decisions they make, and any applicable tax, employment, reporting or submission obligations.

Why we avoid unsupported claims

Payflow SA should earn trust through clear product information rather than promising security or regulatory outcomes that cannot be independently substantiated on the public site.
