Payflow SA Security

Payroll information is sensitive business data. Payflow SA is designed to keep payroll workflows organised while limiting the information exposed through the public website.

What businesses should know

Users should protect account credentials, use appropriate device security and only enter accurate information into the application. Employers remain responsible for deciding what information is collected and ensuring their payroll processes meet applicable legal and privacy requirements.

Privacy and responsible use

Payflow SA should be used as a payroll calculation and workflow aid, not as a replacement for professional tax, accounting or legal advice. Always verify important payroll and statutory outcomes against the applicable South African requirements.

Security questions

If you need information about account security, privacy or data handling, contact Payflow SA through the support channel provided on the website.
